Important Contract Methods

Section titled “Important Contract Methods”

get_st_near_price(&self) -> U128String
Returns how many near one stNEAR is worth.

Usage example, getting stNEAR price using a callback: https://github.com/Narwallets/katherine-fundraising/blob/main/katherine-fundraising-contract/src/internal.rs#L80

get_contract_state(&self) -> GetContractStateResult
Recover multiple parameters related to metapool, including total tokens locked, available liquidity on liquidity pool, stNEAR price and close to 30 others params.
