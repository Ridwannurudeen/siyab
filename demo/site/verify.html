Read a registry record

The page calls latest on the registry contract over the public testnet RPC https://test.rpc.fastnear.com from your browser and shows the record next to the evidence hash you paste. Compute the hash yourself with sha256sum of the evidence file.

The examples in the empty fields are the vendor's latest record, fixtures/registry/testnet-latest-kestrelridge-shop.json, and the sha256 of its evidence document.

Live testnet read

A current read runs only when you choose Read the record. It does not use the website API or sign a transaction.

Ready for a direct read

Enter the registry and subject, and optionally paste an evidence hash. Unavailable reads and absent records do not establish ALLOW.

What the comparison establishes

A matching pasted hash establishes equality with the value returned by the registry. It does not verify a signature, establish the truth of the evidence sources, or prove that the Gate consulted the registry. The live comparison does not compute the hash of an evidence file for you.

The registry and enforcement demonstrations are on testnet. A registry verdict is distinct from a fresh payment check; this browser read sends no payment.
