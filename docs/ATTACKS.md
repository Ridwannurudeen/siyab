The expired submission is a separate refused case. The three protocol refusals have stored RPC error JSON and no receipt, so they have no explorer links.

The aged payee of attack 9 is `examples.testnet`, an account with no contract code created 1825 days before the 2 Nov run (its evidence document `fixtures/evidence/9471fea5*.json`), because the accounts the team controls were under 7 days old that day; every check of its verdict ran and passed, the Gate refused the 2 DMT and nothing was sent to it.
